The paradox

Only 19% of AI users are in the Frontier — high individual + high org readiness.

10% have built strong individual skills but their org can't keep up (Blocked Agency).

65% fear falling behind if they don't adapt quickly. But 45% say it feels safer to focus on current goals than redesign work.

Only 13% say they're rewarded for reinventing work even if results aren't met.

Manager impact

When managers model AI use → +17 pts AI value, +22 pts critical thinking, +30 pts trust in agentic AI.

Source: Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026, May 5 2026. 20,000 workers + 1,800-worker manager study.

Why this matters

Two groups worry most.

Those AI slows down — creatives who find it rigid, yet see it spreading into their field.

Those AI speeds up the most — if tasks take 1/4 the time, what does that mean for headcount?

Implication: treat AI rollouts as a change-management problem. Reassurance and re-scoping matter as much as the tool.

Cutting through the noise

What to weight heavily — and what to discount — when the next AI report or headline lands on your desk.

Weight heavily (signal)
First-party, longitudinal data

Monthly cadence measuring actual usage — Anthropic's Economic Index now publishes monthly.

Combined qual + quant

Worker-experience surveys layered onto usage data show change before it reaches official labor statistics.

Sector-level exposure measures

% of tasks performed with AI by occupation is the closest thing to a leading indicator we have.

Decisions visibly being made

Procurement standards, regulator filings, real layoff/hiring patterns by job family.

Discount (noise)
Vendor benchmark claims

"Our model is 10× faster" tells you nothing about adoption, retention, or value capture.

Single-anecdote layoff headlines

One company replacing N roles is news, not evidence. Look for sector-wide patterns.

Single-source productivity claims

Anthropic's 80% in-chat estimate vs. RCTs at –19% to +56%. Triangulate (see the table above).

Existential debates

Engaging but rarely decision-relevant. Filter to: what would you do differently next quarter?
